Privacy

Your details stay with us

This page explains what happens to the details you send through the enquiry form on this website.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • The service you are interested in, and any sizes or notes you type.

Why we use it

Only to call or message you back about your enquiry, and to prepare a quotation if you ask for one.

Who sees it

Only the Safe View team. We do not sell your details or share them for advertising. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

As long as it is needed for your enquiry, your order and our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time. Contact us using the phone number or email on our contact section.
